**ID prefixes:**
- **Q**: blocking question answered by the PI.
- **F**: fixed by [RR] or by the PI. Changing one requires a new `A` entry.
- **E**: engineering decision (implementation; low scientific impact).
- **O**: open research decision; needs pilot evidence.

**Status values:** `FIXED`, `PROPOSED`, `ACCEPTED`, `ACCEPTED IN PRINCIPLE`, `REJECTED`, `DEFERRED (→ when)`, `RESOLVED (date, evidence)`.

**Rule.** No `O` decision may be resolved by looking at controller-test (meta-test) outcomes. Every resolution cites its evidence: a run ID, a table or a figure.

### A09 — H1, original and corrected

- **Original [RR]:** $H_1:\ \operatorname{Var}(\Delta M_a\mid X)>0$. This is the *conditional* (unexplained) variance at fixed $X$, which is the opposite of the intended claim.
- **Corrected:** heterogeneity means the *expected* benefit differs between contexts beyond what shot noise explains, *and* the best action differs between contexts:
  - $\operatorname{Var}_c\big(\mathbb E[\Delta_a\mid c]\big)$ is materially larger than the within-context shot-replicate variance (variance components with model ID as a random effect);
  - $a^*(c)=\arg\max_a(\Delta_a(c)-\lambda C_a(c))$ is not constant across contexts.

### A10 — H3, original and corrected

- **Original [RR]:** $M_{QT}\ge M_{Always}-\epsilon$ and $C_{QT}<C_{Always}$. If every mitigation effect is smaller than ε, the *Never* policy satisfies this trivially.
- **Corrected.** Q-TRACE succeeds on H3 only if **all** of these hold, with ε and the minimum meaningful saving pre-registered before the locked evaluation:
  1. **Non-inferior performance.** Q-TRACE is non-inferior to the appropriate high-resource reference (Always-mitigate / best fixed action), within ε.
  2. **Lower resources.** Q-TRACE's total resource consumption, *including its probe*, is statistically and meaningfully lower than the reference's.
  3. **A gap to close.** The reference itself materially outperforms Never. Otherwise H3 is *not applicable*: there is no mitigation benefit to select, and that is reported as such.

  Criterion 3 is retained from the original `A10` proposal. Criteria 1–2 alone would still let Never pass trivially when all effects are small.

  **Confirmed by the PI (Phase 1 approval):** all three conditions apply. If the high-resource reference does not clearly outperform Never under a given condition, H3 is marked **not applicable** for that condition, and this is reported, not hidden. A finding that mitigation itself provides little useful benefit is a valid result.
